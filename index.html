How do I know where the leakage is coming from?+

Damp patches, peeling paint and water marks can offer clues, but the source may be different from the visible damage. A site assessment helps identify the likely cause.

Do you provide terrace and bathroom waterproofing?+

Yes. AquaFind lists terrace/roof and bathroom waterproofing among its service areas. The recommended treatment depends on the site condition.

Can I share photos before booking an inspection?+

Yes. You can send photos and a short description through WhatsApp to begin the discussion.

How can I get an estimate?+

Share your location, affected area and project details. The scope and estimate can be discussed after understanding the site requirements.
